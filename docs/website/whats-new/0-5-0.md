# What's new in 0.5.0

**Included in Free.** WB Polls has no paid version.

No new permission. Copy the site kit again after you update.

- **Fits Cloudflare's request limit.** On Cloudflare each plugin call may make 10 requests to the site. Every poll page, vote, admin screen and the post editor's Poll panel now stays within it on a busy site, so a guest vote no longer shows "Something went wrong" after it was saved, and the Polls screen no longer fails.
- **Running totals.** Each poll keeps its vote counts, updated in the same step as the vote. Results show in one read whether a poll has ten votes or ten thousand. The stored votes stay the record: **Recount votes** on the Results screen counts a poll again from them.
- **Polls from earlier versions.** A poll created before 0.5.0 is counted the first time someone opens it. Up to 100 votes are counted at once. A bigger poll is counted over two or three views and shows "Results are being counted" (on your site) or **Counting** (in the admin) until every answer is counted, never a total that is not finished. See [Results and voters](../features/results.md#polls-from-before-050).
- **Deleting a big poll.** The poll disappears from your site and the Polls list at once. Its votes, including the names on a named poll, are removed in the background: a hundred right away, then a hundred each time you open the Polls screen, Results, the edit form or the dashboard.
- **Open and close times.** A poll opens and closes by its dates on every view. Nothing is saved when a visitor happens to be first to see it.
- **Delete my votes for very active members.** A member with votes in hundreds of polls is erased in a few steps the My votes page runs by itself.
- **Named voters list.** Results pages the named list by member, so one member's choices never split across two pages.
- **Find by question.** A search reads up to 300 polls at a time; **Next** searches older ones.

Copy the site kit again after you update: the poll shows "Results are being counted" while a poll from an earlier version is counted, and My votes repeats a long erase by itself.
