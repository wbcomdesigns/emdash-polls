# Use cases

**Included in Free.** WB Polls has no paid version.

Here are common ways to use WB Polls. Each one lists the settings to pick and what visitors then see. Your developer must first add the poll slot to your templates. See [Posts and pages](posts-and-pages.md).

## Ask what to write next on every post

Put one question at the end of every article.

1. Open **New poll**.
2. **Question:** `What should we write about next?`
3. Add your answers, for example `Beginner guides` and `Case studies`.
4. **Where it shows:** One content type, then pick **Posts**.
5. Choose **Create poll**, then **Actions**, then **Open**.

Visitors see the same question under every post. You read the total in **Results**. The [first poll](first-poll.md) page walks through this one in full.

## Put a poll on one article

Use this when a question only fits one post.

1. Open that post in admin.
2. Expand the **Poll** panel in the right sidebar.
3. Fill in the question and answers.
4. Leave **Open now so visitors can vote** on.

Only that post shows the poll. A poll on one page wins over a poll set for all posts, so this post shows its own question and not the shared one. See [Where a poll shows](../features/where-polls-show.md).

## Put a poll on the About page

Use the same **Poll** panel on the page.

1. Open the About page in admin.
2. Use the **Poll** panel to create the poll.

Visitors see it on that page only. This needs the page template to have the poll slot. See [Posts and pages](posts-and-pages.md).

## Show results after voting

Pick this when you want an honest vote first.

- **When to show results:** After voting.

Before voting, visitors see the answers and the line "Results will show after you vote." After they vote, they see bars with the totals.

## Keep results hidden until the poll closes

Pick this for a contest or a final decision.

- **When to show results:** After the poll closes.
- **Closes on:** pick the last day.

Visitors vote and then see "You voted." Once the poll closes, everyone sees the final results. See [Results and voters](../features/results.md).

## Polls for members only

Pick this when each person should vote once.

- **Who can vote:** Signed-in members only.

Guests see the poll and are asked to sign in. Guest votes are counted by internet address or a browser code, and a private window can get around the browser code. Members-only is the reliable choice for one vote per person.

Two optional settings help here:

- **Show each member's choice to the site owner:** you see who chose what in **Results**. Visitors are told before they vote. Guests stay anonymous.
- **Members may change their vote while the poll is open:** members can pick again.

Members can also review their votes on the [My votes](../features/my-votes.md) page.
