# Introduction

**Included in Free.** WB Polls has no paid version.

WB Polls lets you ask your visitors a question and show the results live, on any page of your EmDash site.

![A poll at the end of a blog post, asking what to write about next, above the post's reviews](../images/poll-vote.webp)

## What you can do

- Ask a question with 2 to 10 answers. Visitors pick one, or several if you allow it.
- Choose who can vote: anyone, or only people signed in to your site.
- Choose when results show: always, after a visitor votes, or only after the poll closes.
- Show a poll on one page, on every post, or across the whole site.
- Open a poll now, schedule it for later, and set a day it closes by itself.
- Read the results in admin. On a named poll, see what each member chose.
- Let members see the polls they voted in, and delete their own votes.

## How it fits together

WB Polls has two parts:

| Part | What it does | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The plugin | Stores polls and votes, and gives you the Polls screens in admin | Installed from the EmDash registry |
| The site kit | Draws the poll on your pages and the My votes page | A few files your developer copies into the site once |

EmDash plugins from the registry run in a safe sandbox and cannot add pages or styles to your site by themselves. That is why the visible part comes as a site kit. Your developer copies it once, and after that everything is managed from admin.
