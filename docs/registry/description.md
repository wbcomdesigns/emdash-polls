WB Polls lets a site owner ask a question and show live results on any EmDash page.

## What you get

- Create a poll with two to ten answers, one choice or several.
- Open it when you are ready, close it when voting should stop, or set a close time.
- Choose who can vote: anyone, or signed-in members only.
- Choose when results show: always, after a vote, or after the poll closes.
- Choose anonymous totals, or named results the site owner can see.
- Attach a poll to one page, to every entry of a content type, or to the whole site. A developer can also place a specific poll by id.
- Copy a poll into a new draft with no votes.

## How it fits together

The plugin is the engine: storage, voting routes and the admin screens. An EmDash plugin cannot add pages, so the visible poll ships as a site kit you copy in once. See Installation.

Guests can vote when you allow anyone. Members vote as their signed-in account. Only you create and manage polls.
